Sanctions & Restricted Regions

Effective date: 21 July 2026  |  Last updated: 21 July 2026

1. Overview

MidMightBit Games is committed to complying with all applicable international sanctions laws and export control regulations. Although Sol Card Games is an offline card-game app with no financial functionality, we take our compliance obligations seriously. Use of the App is not permitted by individuals or entities located in, resident in, or subject to the jurisdiction of sanctioned or restricted territories as described on this page.

If you are located in or are a national or resident of any jurisdiction listed in Section 3 below, you are prohibited from using Sol Card Games.

4. How Screening Works

Sol Card Games performs a best-efforts check of a user's jurisdiction at runtime using:

  • SIM card country code — read from the device's TelephonyManager (ISO network country code of the registered network)
  • Device locale — the system locale configured by the user

If either signal indicates that the device is associated with a restricted jurisdiction, the App will restrict access to its features. This is a technical control only and does not constitute a guarantee of perfect enforcement. No location data is transmitted to any server.

These checks are passive, occur entirely on-device, and do not involve GPS, network-based location, or any data collection.

5. User Representations

By using Sol Card Games, you represent and warrant that:

  • You are not located in, a resident of, or subject to the jurisdiction of any restricted territory listed in Section 3

  • You are not acting on behalf of any person or entity in a restricted jurisdiction or on a sanctions list
  • Your use of the App does not violate any applicable sanctions law or export control regulation
