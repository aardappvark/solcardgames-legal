3. Description of the Application

Sol Card Games is an offline collection of ten classic card games for Android devices, optimised for the Solana Seeker. It includes Klondike Solitaire, Spider, FreeCell, Hearts, Spades, Euchre, Gin Rummy, Poker (Texas Hold'em), Cribbage, and War. Games are played solo or against computer-controlled (AI) opponents. The App also includes optional statistics, achievements, and a daily challenge, all stored locally on your device.

Sol Card Games is a single-player entertainment application only. It is NOT a gambling product, NOT a wallet application, NOT a financial services platform, and NOT a decentralised finance (DeFi) protocol. It has no multiplayer or online play. It offers an optional wallet sign-in that reads only your public key for display; it never accesses, moves, holds, or manages any cryptocurrency, tokens, or funds, and never touches your private key.
