Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 February 2025 · Last updated: 17 February 2025 · Version 1.0
Summary: Sol Card Games is a fully offline card game. It requests zero permissions, makes zero network requests, and collects zero personal data. All game data (settings, statistics, achievements) is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and is never transmitted anywhere.

1. Data Controller

Sol Card Games is developed and maintained by MidMightBit Games, an independent developer operating under Australian law.

  • Entity: MidMightBit Games
  • Jurisdiction: Australia
  • Privacy contact: aardappvark@proton.me
  • Package name: com.solcards.app

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the data controller is the developer identified above. If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom and have questions about data protection, please contact us at the email address above.

2. What Data Sol Card Games Stores Locally

Sol Card Games is designed with a zero-collection, local-only architecture. The app operates entirely offline with no internet permission and no network capability whatsoever. All data remains exclusively on your device in app-private storage (Android SharedPreferences). No data is transmitted to any server, because we do not operate any servers and the app has no ability to connect to any network.

None of this data is personal information. The data stored by Sol Card Games consists entirely of game configuration and gameplay statistics. No names, identifiers, accounts, or personal information of any kind are stored.

2.2 Data we do NOT collect

Sol Card Games collects absolutely no personal data. The following is a comprehensive list of data types we do not collect, store, process, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Device identifiers, Android Advertising ID, or hardware serial numbers
  • IP addresses (the app has no internet permission and cannot make network requests)
  • Location data of any kind (GPS, network-based, or otherwise)
  • Usage analytics, behavioural tracking, session data, or telemetry
  • Cookies, browser history, or web tracking data
  • Biometric data
  • Health, financial, or demographic data
  • Contact lists, call logs, SMS, camera, or microphone data
  • Wallet addresses, private keys, seed phrases, or any cryptocurrency data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Advertising or marketing data
  • Any data from other apps on your device

3. Permissions

Sol Card Games requests zero Android permissions. The app does not declare any permissions in its Android manifest, including:

  • No INTERNET permission — the app cannot access the internet
  • No ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — the app cannot detect network connectivity
  • No ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • No CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, or READ_CONTACTS
  • No storage permissions — the app uses only its own private app directory

You can verify this by reviewing the app's permissions in Android Settings → Apps → Sol Card Games → Permissions.

4. No Server-Side Data Storage

Sol Card Games does not operate any backend servers, databases, cloud storage, or analytics infrastructure. We have no server-side systems capable of receiving, storing, or processing any data. The app has no internet permission and is physically incapable of transmitting data off your device. All data exists exclusively in your device's app-private storage and is inaccessible to other applications.

5. No Third-Party Services

Sol Card Games does not integrate any third-party services whatsoever. Specifically, the app includes:

  • No analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or similar)
  • No advertising SDKs (no AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, or similar)
  • No crash reporting SDKs (no Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag, or similar)
  • No social media SDKs
  • No attribution or tracking SDKs
  • No in-app purchase SDKs
  • No push notification services
  • No remote configuration services

Because no third-party SDKs are included, no third party receives any data from the app.

6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect locally stored data:

  • App-private storage: All SharedPreferences files are stored in the app's private directory, inaccessible to other apps on non-rooted devices
  • No remote attack surface: With no internet permission, no server infrastructure, and no network capability, there is no remote vector through which data could be accessed or breached
  • No data in transit: Since the app never transmits data, there is no data-in-transit to intercept

7. Children's Privacy

Sol Card Games is rated for ages 13 and above. However, because the app collects absolutely no data from any user — regardless of age — there is no risk of collecting children's personal information. The app does not:

  • Collect any personal information from children or adults
  • Require account creation or sign-in
  • Enable communication between users
  • Contain advertising or in-app purchases
  • Link to external websites or services

The app is fully compliant with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU Age Appropriate Design Code, and Australia's Online Safety Act requirements for children's services, as it collects no data whatsoever.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device and is retained only for as long as the app is installed. You can delete all Sol Card Games data at any time by:

  • Clearing app data: Android Settings → Apps → Sol Card Games → Storage → Clear Data (resets all settings, statistics, and achievements)
  • Uninstalling the app: Removes all Sol Card Games data from your device completely

Since no data is stored on any server, deletion from your device constitutes complete and permanent deletion. There is no data to request deletion of from us, because we never had it in the first place.

9. Your Rights

9.1 European Economic Area & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the following rights under the GDPR. Because Sol Card Games collects no personal data, these rights are inherently satisfied:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — We do not hold any personal data about you. All app data is on your device and under your control.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — You may modify any game settings or data directly within the app.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — You may delete all data by clearing app data or uninstalling (see Section 8).
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18) — No data processing occurs beyond local on-device storage.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — All data is stored locally in standard Android SharedPreferences on your device.
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — There is no data processing to object to, as we collect nothing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
