Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 July 2026  |  Last updated: 22 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MidMightBit Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Sol Card Games mobile application ("App"). We have written this policy to be plain and direct: Sol Card Games collects no personal data, transmits nothing to external servers, and operates entirely on your device. We are committed to your privacy by design and by default.

Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with any part of it, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Data Controller

MidMightBit Games
Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
Contact: aardappvark@proton.me

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and equivalent legislation, MidMightBit Games is the data controller for any information processed in connection with the App. As described throughout this policy, we process no personal data through any external system.

3. Data We Collect

We collect absolutely no personal data. Sol Card Games does not collect, record, transmit, share, sell, or otherwise process any personal information about you, your device, your location, your behaviour, or your identity.

There are no servers and no databases operated by us, and there are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the App. The only data exchange that can occur is the optional wallet connection you may initiate in Settings (see Section 7), which talks to your own wallet app — not to us.

4. Local Storage

The App stores the following information locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences mechanism:

  • Your game statistics (such as games played and games won, per game)
  • Your daily-challenge progress and streak
  • Your achievement / unlock progress
  • Your in-app settings and preferences (such as sound, theme, and difficulty)
  • A flag recording that you have accepted the in-app disclaimer
  • The public key of a Solana wallet, only if you choose to connect one (see Section 7)

This data is stored solely on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. It is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App.

5. No Network Requests

Sol Card Games makes zero outbound network requests during normal operation. The App does not require an internet connection for any core feature. There are no calls to analytics endpoints, no crash-reporting payloads, no remote configuration fetches, and no advertising network traffic.

The only network activity occurs if you use the optional wallet connection (Section 7): the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter communicates with your installed wallet app to complete Sign In With Solana. No gameplay data or personal information is transmitted to us at any time — we operate no servers.

6. No Accounts Required

Sol Card Games requires no account registration, no email address, no username, no password, and no sign-in of any kind. You can use every feature of the App without identifying yourself to us in any way.

7. Wallet Connection (Optional)

Sol Card Games offers an optional feature to connect a Solana wallet from Settings using the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA) and Sign In With Solana (SIWS). Connecting a wallet is entirely optional — every game is fully playable without one.

  • Only your public key (wallet address) is read and stored locally in SharedPreferences.
  • Your private key and seed phrase are never accessed, read, stored, or transmitted by Sol Card Games under any circumstances.
  • Sol Card Games does not request transaction signing, does not initiate any on-chain operations, and does not access, hold, or move any funds or tokens.
  • Your public key is used only to show which wallet is linked, and is never sent to us or any third party.
  • You may disconnect at any time from Settings, which removes the stored public key from your device.

8. Permissions

The App declares the following Android permissions and uses them solely as described:

PermissionPurpose
VIBRATEProvides short haptic feedback for in-game actions (for example, dealing or playing a card).
INTERNETRequired by the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter for the optional wallet connection (Section 7). It is not used for analytics, advertising, or any other purpose, and no network activity occurs unless you choose to connect a wallet.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDeclared by the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter library to check connectivity during the optional wallet connection. Used only for the wallet feature.
REORDER_TASKSDeclared by the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter library to switch to your wallet app and back during the optional wallet connection. Used only for the wallet feature.

The App declares no location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or telephony permission. The sanctions region check (see our Sanctions page) reads only the SIM's country code and the device locale, both of which are available without any permission, entirely on-device. No permission is used to collect, read, or transmit personal data to us.

9. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

Sol Card Games contains no analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude), no advertising SDKs (such as AdMob or Meta Audience Network), and no tracking or profiling tools of any kind. Your usage behaviour, session duration, feature interactions, and device fingerprint are never recorded or processed.

10. Children's Privacy

Sol Card Games is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the App collects no personal data from any user, this commitment is satisfied structurally. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information in connection with the App, please contact us at aardappvark@proton.me and we will take appropriate action.

11. International Data Transfers

Because Sol Card Games collects no personal data and operates no servers, no international data transfers occur. Your information never crosses any border in connection with this App.

13. Data Retention

The only data associated with your use of Sol Card Games — your locally stored game statistics, progress, preferences, and the public key of a connected wallet (if any) — is retained on your device for as long as the App is installed. A connected wallet's public key is also removed as soon as you disconnect it. This data is automatically and permanently deleted when you uninstall the App. We retain no copy of any such data.
