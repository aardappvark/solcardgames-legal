Sol Card Games

Built for Solana Seeker

Ten classic card games — offline, no gambling.

Sol Card Games brings ten classic card games to your Solana Seeker in one calm, dark app: Klondike Solitaire, Spider, FreeCell, Hearts, Spades, Euchre, Gin Rummy, Poker (Texas Hold'em), Cribbage, and War. Play solitaire on your own or take on AI opponents, track your stats and achievements, and tackle a fresh daily challenge. Every game works fully offline with no account and no sign-up; the only things that ever leave the device are optional, anonymous aggregate usage analytics (switchable off in Settings) and the optional wallet features described below.

Sol Card Games is for entertainment only. No real money or cryptocurrency is wagered, won, or lost through gameplay. The single paid feature is an optional one-time Pro unlock (0.01 SOL or a discounted 10 SKR) that you sign from your own wallet; the app never takes custody of funds and never touches your private key.

Features

Ten Classic Games

Solitaire (Klondike), Spider, FreeCell, Hearts, Spades, Euchre, Gin Rummy, Poker, Cribbage, and War — all in one app.

Solo & vs AI

Play solitaire games on your own or take on computer-controlled opponents. No multiplayer, no online play.

Daily Challenge & Streaks

A fresh challenge every day. Build a streak and unlock achievements as you play.

Stats & Achievements

Track games played, games won, and milestones — all stored locally on your device.

Plays Offline

Every game works fully offline, even in aeroplane mode. The network is used only for optional anonymous analytics and the optional wallet features.

Entertainment Only

Not a gambling app. No real money, no cryptocurrency, no items of value in gameplay — chips and scores are just for fun.

Optional Wallet Sign-In

Connect a Solana wallet from Settings via Sign In With Solana. Public key only — no access to your funds, and the only transaction ever offered is the optional Pro unlock you sign yourself. Disconnect anytime.

Optional Pro Unlock

A one-time purchase of 0.01 SOL, or a discounted 10 SKR, paid on-chain from your own wallet. Adds unlimited hints, advanced stats, the Aurora theme and a supporter badge. All ten games stay free; Pro restores by reconnecting the paying wallet.
